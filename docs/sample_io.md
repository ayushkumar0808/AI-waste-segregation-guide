# EcoSort: Sample Inputs and Expected Outputs

This document records 5 representative sample scenarios testing different waste streams under India's Solid Waste Management (SWM) Rules 2016.

---

### Sample 1: Soiled Food Packaging
- **User Input:** `"used pizza box with grease"`
- **Category:** `Dry / Recyclable (Special Caution)`
- **Bin Colour:** `Blue Bin (Clean parts) / Wet or RDF (Greasy parts)`
- **Reason:** Cardboard saturated with food grease and oils cannot be processed by paper recycling hydropulpers.
- **Eco Tip:** Tear off the clean top cardboard lid for the blue recycling bin; send the greasy base for composting or dry reject/RDF.
- **Source Used:** *SWM Rules 2016: Section 2: Dry Waste (Recyclable Non-Biodegradable)*
- **Safety Alert:** None.

---

### Sample 2: Household Electronic Waste
- **User Input:** `"old mobile phone battery"`
- **Category:** `E-Waste`
- **Bin Colour:** `Yellow / Orange Bin (or E-Waste drop-box)`
- **Reason:** Contains hazardous lithium/heavy metals and chemical electrolytes regulated under E-Waste (Management) Rules.
- **Eco Tip:** Tape the terminal contacts with masking tape to prevent accidental electrical short circuits, and deposit at campus e-waste drives.
- **Source Used:** *SWM Rules 2016: Section 4: E-Waste (Electronic & Electrical Waste)*
- **Safety Alert:** ⚠️ **Hazardous/E-Waste warning displayed.**

---

### Sample 3: Organic Kitchen Waste
- **User Input:** `"banana peels and leftover vegetables"`
- **Category:** `Wet Waste`
- **Bin Colour:** `Green Bin`
- **Reason:** Organic, biodegradable material that naturally decomposes via aerobic composting or bio-methanation.
- **Eco Tip:** Keep wet waste free from plastic wraps and compost locally to produce natural plant fertilizer.
- **Source Used:** *SWM Rules 2016: Section 1: Wet Waste (Biodegradable / Compostable)*
- **Safety Alert:** None.

---

### Sample 4: Expired Domestic Medication
- **User Input:** `"expired paracetamol tablet strip"`
- **Category:** `Domestic Hazardous`
- **Bin Colour:** `Red Bin or Marked Hazardous Box`
- **Reason:** Active pharmaceutical compounds pose chemical toxicity risks to groundwater and sanitation workers if dumped into standard landfills.
- **Eco Tip:** Never flush pharmaceuticals down sinks or toilets; return them during hospital drug take-back days.
- **Source Used:** *SWM Rules 2016: Section 3: Domestic Hazardous Waste*
- **Safety Alert:** ⚠️ **Hazardous/Biomedical advisory displayed.**

---

### Sample 5: Recyclable Beverage Packaging
- **User Input:** `"empty plastic soda bottle"`
- **Category:** `Dry / Recyclable`
- **Bin Colour:** `Blue Bin`
- **Reason:** PET plastic is high-grade recyclable material intended for mechanical sorting at Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs).
- **Eco Tip:** Rinse out residual sugar, crush the bottle flat, and screw the cap back on before placing it in the blue bin.
- **Source Used:** *SWM Rules 2016: Section 2: Dry Waste (Recyclable Non-Biodegradable)*
- **Safety Alert:** None.
